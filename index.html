Apps that do one job well.

Clever Ape Studio makes small iPhone apps for candle makers, youth soccer coaches and anyone who keeps a log. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What they do

Pourwell weighs out a batch

Give it the container, the candle count and the fragrance load. It returns wax and oil to the gram, and flags a load outside the range for your wax.

Pourwell prices a candle

Enter what the wax, oil, vessels and wicks cost you. It gives the cost of one candle, your margin at your price, and a wholesale and retail price.

Benchly shares out the minutes

Everyone gets a fair turn. Set the squad, the players on the field and the length of the game: Benchly redoes this sum at every change.

Benchly runs the sideline

A game clock says when the next change is due, who goes on and for whom. One tap makes the change, and the minutes stay fair.

Vyber changes what you point at

Tap any part of an applet and write what should change. Here, “make this bigger” turns a water tracker's small daily goal into a large number.

Vyber builds the applet you describe

Trackers, logs, timers and simple games, made from a sentence. This workout log was built in Vyber, and what it records stays on the phone.
